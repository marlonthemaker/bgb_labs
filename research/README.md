# Research

This directory separates durable research authority from individual studies.

| Path | Role | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`canon/`](canon/README.md) | Accepted native-adoption strategy, method, governance, and productization rules. | Active authority. |
| [`ailitw/studies/iberia-2026/`](ailitw/studies/iberia-2026/README.md) | Proposed real-world follow-up to Hotel Shoreline. | Pre-pilot; no public execution authorized. |

Runtime packages must not depend on research files. Raw or private study
artifacts must be ignored or access-controlled; sanitized, licensed evidence may
be committed only when the study protocol and retention rules permit it.

Historical source material removed during consolidation remains available in
Git history through the parent of the consolidation commit. The disposition is
recorded in [`D003`](../docs/decisions/D003-repository-research-consolidation.md).
