# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines how bomgoodbueno should test commercial demand without outrunning the evidence, compromising research independence, or building product infrastructure prematurely.

The commercial strategy is service-led and research-backed. Specific buyers, channels, offer details, prices, and conversion assumptions remain hypotheses until validated.

## Commercial Thesis

Organizations deploying agents to multilingual users may need more than translation quality checks. They may need evidence that users can understand, trust, and complete real workflows across languages and local contexts.

bomgoodbueno can create value by helping those organizations:

- discover capability gaps before or after launch;
- identify where failures enter the agent lifecycle;
- distinguish language symptoms from workflow, policy, retrieval, tool, or product failures;
- prioritize targeted interventions;
- retest under matched conditions;
- make narrower, better-supported launch and support claims.

This thesis is not considered commercially validated until customers use the work to make decisions and demonstrate willingness to pay or commit meaningful access.

## Market Position

Do not position bomgoodbueno as:

- translation quality assurance;
- generic AI evaluation;
- broad AI transformation consulting;
- a multilingual model leaderboard;
- certification or compliance approval;
- a hotel or customer-support software vendor.

The intended position is:

> Evidence-backed native adoption analysis for organizations deploying agentic AI across languages and local contexts.

## First Customer Hypotheses

### Primary Wedge: Customer Support And CX Agents

Potential buyers:

- support or customer-experience leaders;
- support operations and automation leaders;
- product leaders responsible for AI support agents.

Potential pain:

- multilingual users receive lower resolution quality;
- escalation, consent, trust, or policy behavior differs by language;
- teams cannot connect language failures to operational metrics or fixes;
- launch claims exceed journey-level evidence.

This is the first wedge hypothesis because workflows and outcomes are usually concrete. It is not yet an accepted market fact.

### Secondary Wedge: SaaS AI Product Teams

Potential buyers are product and AI leaders serving users across markets who need release, readiness, or regression evidence for agentic workflows.

### Influencers And Partners

Localization, global-content, language-technology, AI-governance, trust, evaluation, and CX advisory teams may influence demand or become channel partners. They should not be assumed to own budget without evidence.

## Offer Sequence

### Stage 0: Problem Discovery

Allowed while Hotel Shoreline finishes and before the field-study gate:

- customer interviews;
- workflow and incident discovery;
- terminology and value-proposition testing;
- review of what artifacts buyers can share;
- testing which business decisions the method could support.

Do not claim the method is proven or offer live-agent conclusions at this stage.

### Stage 1: Cooperative Snapshot

After the controlled method and responsible-access rules are ready, a cooperative organization may receive a narrow Native Adoption Snapshot:

- one low-risk or permissioned workflow;
- limited language and locale scope;
- observed gaps and evidence summary;
- representation and limitation statement;
- recommended next tests;
- no certification or broad launch claim.

The snapshot is a customer-acquisition hypothesis, not an obligation to perform unlimited free research.

### Stage 2: Native Adoption Analysis

The first expected paid service may include:

- one to three critical workflows;
- a small declared language and locale scope;
- reviewed semantic contracts or customer cases;
- baseline evaluation;
- lifecycle diagnosis and failure map;
- intervention recommendation;
- one treatment and retest where access permits;
- executive decision memo;
- technical artifact and implementation handoff;
- explicit supported and unsupported claims.

The exact scope, duration, and price must be tested through design-partner and customer discovery.

### Stage 3: Native Adoption Readiness Program

A recurring program is justified only when customers need repeated language expansion, release regression, treatment review, or governance support and the delivery model is demonstrably repeatable.

### Stage 4: Software-Supported Product

Product surfaces follow the ladder in
[`PRODUCTIZATION.md`](PRODUCTIZATION.md). Software is built to reduce repeated
delivery cost or error, not to manufacture the appearance of market maturity.

## Research-Led Acquisition

The preferred growth motion creates useful evidence before requesting a large commitment:

```text
customer discovery or responsible research
  -> narrow evidence-backed handoff
  -> buyer recognizes a specific decision or risk
  -> scoped paid analysis
  -> treatment and retest
  -> repeatable method and artifact patterns
  -> earned product capability
```

Possible channels after their relevant gates are satisfied:

- cooperative design partners;
- private snapshots for qualified organizations;
- aggregate AILITW research;
- public methods, templates, or synthetic examples;
- partnerships with CX, localization, trust, governance, or agent-platform teams;
- targeted outreach linked to a specific observed or declared workflow problem.

Public-system research must follow [`FIELD_RESEARCH.md`](FIELD_RESEARCH.md).
GTM urgency does not authorize live testing, scraping, named criticism, or
publication.

## Value Evidence

Commercial validation should track whether the work:

- reveals failures the organization did not already understand;
- changes a launch, product, support, or governance priority;
- produces an intervention the technical team can act on;
- improves a matched outcome without unacceptable regressions;
- earns access to a deeper workflow or a repeat engagement;
- becomes faster and more consistent to deliver;
- produces reusable cases, diagnostics, or treatment patterns.

Activity metrics such as messages sent, reports produced, or meetings held are useful operationally but do not prove customer value.

## Falsification Signals

Reconsider the offer, wedge, or strategy if:

- buyers consistently treat the problem as low priority;
- teams only want translation QA or generic model evaluation;
- findings do not affect business or product decisions;
- useful evidence cannot be collected within acceptable access and privacy limits;
- interventions remain too bespoke to repeat;
- buyers require a mature hosted platform before they value the analysis;
- the method cannot separate useful signal from evaluator or fixture noise;
- trust and governance work adds process without improving decisions.

## Commercial Guardrails

- Be generous with scoped insight and precise about what remains untested.
- Never imply certification, universal language support, or broad superiority.
- Do not publicly shame organizations or use avoidable adversarial framing.
- Do not handle private user data without explicit agreement and controls.
- Do not let free work become unbounded consulting.
- Keep paid research findings independent from the customer's preferred narrative.
- Revalidate dated market statistics, target lists, competitor claims, and prices before using them externally.
