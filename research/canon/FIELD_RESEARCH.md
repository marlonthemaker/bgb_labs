# Field Research

AILITW is bomgoodbueno's public, cooperative, and operator-authorized
live-agent research track. It studies whether deployed agents preserve useful,
reliable, and appropriate operational capability across languages and locales.

## Evidence boundary

The program distinguishes three environments:

| Environment | Valid evidence | Invalid inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Controlled fictional system | Causal baseline/intervention evidence against deterministic truth. | That the same treatment fixes a named production agent. |
| Public production surface | Externally observable behavior under recorded conditions. | Hidden implementation, root cause, or operator intent. |
| Operator-authorized system | Matched baseline/remedy evidence within the approved boundary. | General effectiveness outside tested conditions. |

These domains may be connected by a versioned observable-signature analogue;
they must not be pooled into one rate or described as equivalent experiments.

## Entry gates

Public observation begins only when:

- the controlled method and evidence pipeline are reproducible;
- the target, surface, current terms, access basis, and locale support are
  documented;
- cases, expected behavior, exclusions, and stop rules are versioned;
- privacy, retention, screenshot, notification, and publication boundaries are
  explicit;
- human-participation and ethics requirements have an appropriate documented
  determination;
- reviewer capacity matches the language and construct being evaluated; and
- the expected value justifies interacting with the system.

The first Iberia milestone is a non-ranking 27-cell feasibility pilot selected
after the access audit. The 70-seed catalog is a candidate library, not an
automatic execution manifest.

## Responsible testing boundary

Without explicit target authorization, the study must not:

- bypass authentication, access controls, anti-bot controls, or rate limits;
- extract secrets, private data, hidden instructions, or another user's data;
- purchase, book, pay, or mutate an account, order, reservation, or service;
- overload, disrupt, deceive, exploit, or degrade the system;
- send high-volume automation or an autonomous crawler;
- transfer a research interaction to an unwitting human support worker;
- retain or publish artifacts beyond the documented permission and necessity;
- present unsupported language conditions as failures; or
- generalize a narrow observation to an entire language, market, or product.

Prefer manual, low-volume, reversible observation. Stop escalation cases once
a valid handoff route is offered and before a human joins.

## Minimum study record

A field study records:

- target, owner, surface, access basis, terms evidence, and audit timestamp;
- protocol, cases, versions, run window, budgets, and reference conditions;
- language/locale support, provenance, review, and representation limits;
- immutable observations, artifact hashes, exclusions, and retention policy;
- evaluator/rubric versions, disagreement, adjudication, and uncertainty;
- supported and unsupported claims;
- notification, factual-correction, disclosure, and publication decisions; and
- links to any controlled analogue without asserting identical root cause.

## Publication and operator handoff

Lead with aggregate, scoped patterns. System-specific reporting requires the
approved naming and publication policy, sufficient attributable evidence, a
factual-correction channel, and risk review. Paid work does not buy favorable
findings.

An operator brief separates observations, interpretations, controlled analogue,
proposed remedy, limitations, and requested validation. If an operator engages,
create a new agreement and protocol covering authorization, data, security,
privacy, baseline, treatment, success criteria, stopping, rollback, ownership,
and publication. Public access is never implied authorization to intervene.
