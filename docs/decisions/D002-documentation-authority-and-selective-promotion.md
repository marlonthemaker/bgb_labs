# D002 — Documentation Authority and Selective Promotion

Date: 2026-08-21

Status: superseded by D003 and D004

## Decision

Establish distinct owners for durable canon, decisions, active research,
orientation, execution guidance, and historical provenance. Promote historical
guidance only after reconciling it with current canon, and keep mutable task
status in one declared owner.

## Rationale

Canon v0.2 removed premature platform and product assumptions but also
compressed useful mission, research, product-maturity, commercial, and
governance guidance. Selective promotion retained durable knowledge without
restoring the previous scope problem.

Separating canon, orientation, execution instructions, and task state reduced
the risk that a durable decision, current activity, hypothesis, or historical
record would be mistaken for another class.

## Consequences at the time

- Canon v0.2 was hardened rather than replaced.
- README and agent guidance remained derived orientation and execution layers.
- Dated market claims and target lists remained research inputs.
- Premature platform, graph, registry, API, dashboard, and public-agent growth
  plans remained deferred.
- Major semantic changes required a decision record and corresponding canon
  update.

## Supersession

D003 established the current monorepo paths. D004 retains accepted decision
records in the active tree, makes individual issue metadata the source of
delivery status, and validates derived indexes automatically. This record is
historical context, not current path or task guidance.
