# D001 — Canonical Reset to v0.2

Date: 2026-08-21

Status: superseded by D003 and D004

## Decision

Adopt Canon v0.2 as the active bomgoodbueno authority and archive earlier
Hospeda-first, Language Mesh, and broad platform-first documents as historical
context.

## Rationale

The previous repository contained valuable thinking but too much active surface
area for a pre-MVP proof. The near-term goal was to prove the native-adoption
research method through MR-0 before building platform infrastructure or
committing to a commercial product shape.

## Consequences at the time

- `docs/` became authoritative.
- `archive/` became provenance only.
- Hotel Aurora remained the controlled research fixture.
- Hospeda became historical product context rather than active execution.
- MR-0 became the next gate.

## Supersession

D003 replaced the repository structure, Hotel Shoreline replaced Hotel Aurora,
and the HSD roadmap replaced MR-0. This record remains for decision provenance;
it is not an active instruction.
