# Roadmap

The roadmap defines program phases and promotion gates. Detailed execution state belongs to the active research index, not this document.

## Phase 0 - Canon v0.2 Migration

Status: complete as of 2026-08-21.

Outcome:

- durable `bgb_labs` root;
- prior materials preserved with provenance;
- active Canon v0.2 installed and hardened;
- documentation authority and archive-promotion rules recorded;
- MR-0 ready for controlled execution.

## Phase 1 - MR-0 Controlled Method POC

Status: active.

Sequence:

1. Freeze Hotel Aurora fixture and deterministic tool contracts.
2. Define the case schema and 12 semantic contracts.
3. Author English, `es-ES`, and `pt-PT` variants.
4. Review semantic and native-language equivalence.
5. Run the baseline evaluation.
6. Diagnose lifecycle failure points.
7. Test one targeted intervention and check regressions.
8. Write the MR-0 report and method decisions.

Current task ownership and status live in `../research/mr0/README.md`.

Exit gate:

- the controlled method is reproducible;
- evidence supports scoped conclusions;
- limitations and invalid results are visible;
- the intervention loop produced a useful positive or negative decision;
- required changes before live evaluation are recorded.

## Phase 2 - Native Adoption Protocol v0.1

Codify the measurement, diagnosis, review, evidence, and intervention method that survived MR-0.

Remove fixture-specific assumptions that are not part of the method. Do not generalize beyond the evidence.

## Phase 3 - MR-1 Cooperative Live-Agent POC

Apply the protocol to a cooperative or explicitly permissioned live agent.

Validate access, privacy, evidence capture, customer handoff, and practical decision value without introducing broad public testing.

## Phase 4 - AILITW-001

Begin public-agent research with a constrained, ethical, reproducible target set after the entry gates in `04_AILITW.md` are satisfied.

Start with narrow claims and aggregate or private outputs where named publication is not justified.

## Phase 5 - Native Adoption Analysis

Offer the first service engagement after the method has credible evidence and a cooperative live proof.

Validate:

- buyer and workflow urgency;
- access to useful artifacts;
- decision value beyond translation QA;
- repeatability of diagnosis and handoff;
- willingness to pay;
- research independence and claim boundaries.

## Phase 6 - Repeatable Service And Earned Tooling

Standardize repeated delivery components. Promote product capabilities through the maturity states in `06_PRODUCT_ENGINEERING.md`.

Reports and portable artifacts remain the default surface. Build CLI, SDK, MCP, CI, API, or hosted surfaces only when repeated demand validates each step.

## Phase 7 - Product Or Network Decision

Decide from evidence whether bomgoodbueno should remain service-led, become a software-supported research and assurance firm, build a product platform, establish a benchmark or intelligence network, or pursue a partner model.

This decision requires repeated customer value, defensible methodology, operational repeatability, and responsible data governance. It is not assumed by the current roadmap.

## Expansion Rule

Do not expand languages, systems, market claims, service breadth, or infrastructure merely because the next phase is listed. Each phase requires its own evidence and accepted decision.
