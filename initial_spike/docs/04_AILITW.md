# AILITW

AILITW is the public and live-agent research track.

It studies how deployed agents behave across languages, locales, and native modes of expression under realistic conditions.

## Role In The Roadmap

AILITW is not MR-0.

MR-0 proves the method in a controlled environment first. AILITW follows by applying a mature method to public agents, cooperative customer agents, or responsibly browser-observed workflows.

The intended sequence is:

```text
MR-0 controlled proof
  -> Native Adoption Protocol
  -> MR-1 cooperative live-agent proof
  -> constrained AILITW research
```

## Research Questions

AILITW may investigate:

- whether public support claims match observed workflow capability;
- where cross-language capability first breaks in deployed systems;
- whether realistic journeys reveal failures missed by prompt-only tests;
- whether behavior changes over time, releases, surfaces, or locales;
- which observed patterns justify controlled follow-up or applied work.

AILITW observations do not establish causal mechanisms by themselves.

## Entry Gates

Live-agent work may begin only when:

- the controlled method is reproducible;
- target cases, expected outcomes, and review rules are versioned;
- the access method and relevant terms are understood;
- privacy, rate, retention, notification, and publication boundaries are explicit;
- the expected research value justifies the interaction;
- the study has clear stopping and exclusion rules.

Automation must not be pointed at live public agents until these gates are met.

## Responsible Testing Boundary

AILITW must not:

- bypass authentication, access controls, rate limits, or restrictions;
- attempt to extract secrets, private data, or hidden instructions;
- overload, disrupt, deceive, harass, or exploit a system or its operators;
- submit high-risk or consequential requests without appropriate permission and review;
- publish sensitive details that create avoidable operational or user harm;
- represent a narrow observation as a claim about an entire language, market, or product.

Prefer cooperative, permissioned, low-volume, and reversible study designs. Separate ordinary public observation from permissioned customer evaluation.

## Minimum Study Record

An AILITW study should record:

- target system, surface, owner, and access basis;
- public claims or research question being examined;
- language, locale, community, domain, and representation limits;
- case set, protocol, budget, and run window;
- captured artifacts and retention rules;
- evaluator requirements;
- exclusions, incidents, limitations, and notification decisions;
- supported and unsupported claims.

## Outputs

Potential outputs include:

- scoped observations and failure patterns;
- system or claim snapshots;
- controlled follow-up questions;
- aggregate research reports;
- private handoff packets for cooperative organizations;
- candidate interventions for the Applied Lab;
- reusable cases, rubrics, or public goods when responsible.

Named comparisons, league tables, certification language, and public superiority claims require stronger evidence and governance than exploratory AILITW work.

## Commercial Boundary

AILITW may create credibility and qualified demand, but research findings must remain separable from commercial incentives.

The research-led handoff motion in `09_GO_TO_MARKET.md` cannot override the entry gates or responsible-testing boundary in this document.
