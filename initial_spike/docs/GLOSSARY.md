# Glossary

This glossary defines active Canon v0.2 terms. Archived terms are not active unless listed here or promoted by a decision.

## Active Terms

### AILITW

The public and live-agent research track. It applies a mature native-adoption method to public, cooperative, or otherwise responsibly accessed deployed systems.

### Applied Lab

The function that turns a diagnosed native-adoption failure into a targeted intervention, matched retest, decision, and implementation handoff.

### Baseline

The recorded behavior of the system under test before the declared intervention.

### Canon

The durable operating guidance in `docs/`, as amended by accepted decisions.

### Capability

A behaviorally observable ability, such as preserving a condition, selecting a workflow, calling a tool correctly, escalating appropriately, or communicating a valid outcome.

### Capability Parity

Comparable capability for a declared task and context across a target language or locale and a stated baseline. It is capability-specific, not a universal property of a language.

### Case

One coherent user need requiring an informational or operational outcome. A message may contain zero, one, or several cases.

### Diagnostic Lifecycle

The six stages used to locate the earliest loss of capability: Input, Understand, Decompose, Retrieve/Reason, Act, and Respond.

### Evidence Level

The E0-E5 scale in `03_RESEARCH_METHOD.md` describing how strongly evidence supports a claim.

### Fixture

A controlled set of facts, state, policies, and tool behavior used to make an evaluation reproducible. Hotel Aurora is the MR-0 fixture.

### Finding

A scoped conclusion supported by declared evidence and limitations. A hypothesis or observation is not automatically a finding.

### Ground Truth

The authored and reviewed expected meaning, behavior, outcome, or rubric used to judge a case. Model output cannot define its own ground truth.

### Intervention

A deliberate change intended to address a diagnosed failure, such as a prompt, tool-schema, routing, clarification, retrieval, policy, or response constraint.

### Language Variant

A user-facing realization of a semantic contract in a declared language and locale. Variants may be translated, parallel, native-authored, or market-specific and must identify their source.

### Locale

Region-linked conventions such as dates, currencies, names, addresses, units, forms of address, and formatting. Locale is not a synonym for culture or community.

### Native Adoption

The preservation of practical AI capability when people interact through their own language, locale, register, pragmatic style, institution, and workflow.

### Native Adoption Analysis

The expected first service: a scoped evaluation, diagnosis, intervention recommendation or test, and evidence-backed implementation handoff for a customer's agent or workflow.

### Native Capability

The ability to produce an acceptable outcome in the tested local context, not merely fluent or translated text.

### Parallel, Native, And Market Tests

Three case-source types. Parallel tests preserve a shared semantic contract, native tests are authored directly in the target language, and market tests depend on local institutions or workflows.

### Representation Limits

An explicit statement of which people, dialects, regions, domains, accessibility contexts, systems, or deployment settings a study does not represent.

### Semantic Contract

A language-independent definition of a user's intended need, relevant context, expected behavior, allowed and prohibited outcomes, and evaluation evidence.

### Task

Work required to resolve a case within the evaluated system boundary.

### Task-Critical Information

Information that must survive the workflow for the task to be completed correctly, including entities, quantities, times, conditions, negation, dependencies, exclusions, and authorization requirements.

### Treatment

A versioned implementation of an intervention evaluated against a baseline. The term may be used in experimental records even when no product capability results.

## Product Capability States

The active states are `concept`, `research_backed`, `treatment_backed`, `trust_reviewed`, `pilot_ready`, `package_ready`, and `retired`. Their requirements are defined in `06_PRODUCT_ENGINEERING.md`.

## Historical Or Inactive Terms

### CompanyBench

An archived customer-specific benchmark concept. It is not an active offer or required architecture.

### Hospeda

An archived hotel-product direction. It has no current execution authority. Hotel Aurora does not reactivate Hospeda.

### Language Mesh

An archived architecture and product direction. It is not part of the active roadmap.

### Native Adoption Index

An archived aggregate-score hypothesis. Canon v0.2 reports scoped capabilities and outcomes; no universal index is currently approved.

### Wild, Lab, And Sandbox

An earlier three-track program model. Current canon uses controlled research and MR work, AILITW, Applied Lab, and Product Engineering. Archived track IDs may remain in historical artifacts but should not be introduced into new MR-0 records without a decision.
