# AILITW Market Intelligence

Imported generated reports for AILITW target selection, outreach, growth, and sellability analysis.

These files are active research inputs, not canon.

Use them under the scope and sequencing rules in:

- `../../../docs/04_AILITW.md`
- `../../../docs/09_GO_TO_MARKET.md`
- `../../../docs/10_GOVERNANCE.md`

Before using any content externally:

- verify dated market statistics and source links;
- requalify target companies, products, access methods, and contacts;
- treat prices, outreach quotas, conversion rates, and customer segments as hypotheses;
- do not infer permission to test a live system from its inclusion in a target list;
- keep public and commercial claims narrower than the available evidence.

The source copies are preserved under `../../../archive/conversations/`. Their duplication here makes them available as working inputs; it does not promote them to canon or independently validate their conclusions.
