# Strategy

## Strategic Position

bomgoodbueno should own a narrow but important territory:

> Native adoption of agentic systems.

The lab should not try to compete with frontier model labs, generic evaluation
platforms, translation QA vendors, or enterprise automation suites on their own
terms. It should focus on a harder and more specific question:

> Does a deployed intelligent system preserve capability, policy, safety,
> workflow correctness, and user experience across languages and local contexts?

## Why Now

Agentic systems are moving from chat interfaces into real workflows. They answer
customers, retrieve policy, select tools, modify records, escalate cases, and
coordinate operational outcomes.

At the same time, many vendors market broad language support. But language
support is often declared at the interface level while failures occur deeper in
the system:

- misunderstood intent;
- lost constraints;
- wrong decomposition;
- incorrect tool selection;
- malformed tool arguments;
- policy drift;
- weak local institutional knowledge;
- unnatural register;
- unreliable multilingual judging;
- regressions after model, prompt, retriever, workflow, or tool changes.

The market needs a way to evaluate real deployed systems across languages and
markets, not only base models on abstract benchmarks.

## Where We Play

bomgoodbueno plays at the intersection of:

- multilingual AI evaluation;
- agentic workflow assurance;
- native-language and market-readiness analysis;
- applied research;
- responsible public-system observation;
- enterprise release and regression assurance.

The founding research surface is cross-language capability parity across
English, Spanish, and Portuguese as used in Portugal.

## How We Win

bomgoodbueno wins by making native adoption measurable.

The lab should build methods and infrastructure that can answer:

- What did the system claim to support?
- What public or permissioned surface was tested?
- What capability was tested?
- What semantic case was presented?
- What language and locale were used?
- Did the system interpret the case correctly?
- Did it preserve task-critical information?
- Did it choose the right workflow?
- Did it produce an equivalent outcome?
- Did native users experience the response as appropriate?
- What evidence supports the conclusion?

## Strategic Flywheel

```text
Public and permissioned system observation
  -> native adoption framework refinement
  -> reusable cases and rubrics
  -> AILITW reports
  -> credibility and inbound applied demand
  -> Applied Lab engagements
  -> CompanyBench and assurance workflows
  -> product infrastructure
  -> better and faster research
  -> public goods
```

## AILITW Before SaaS

AILITW should come before a generic SaaS product because the category needs
evidence and methodology before it needs dashboards.

The first software should be scientific infrastructure:

- claim registry;
- system atlas;
- case/task library;
- run capture;
- trace capture;
- human review;
- evidence registry;
- report generation.

This tooling may later become a customer-facing product, but the first customer
is the lab itself.

## Applied Lab Strategy

Applied Lab creates practical offerings from the research system.

Initial applied offers may include:

- Native Adoption Analysis;
- Cross-Language Capability Parity Audit;
- European Market Readiness Review;
- CompanyBench design;
- Agent/Model Assurance;
- Release Regression Assurance;
- Multilingual AI Assurance Report.

The commercial proposition is:

> Supported is not the same as equivalent. We test whether your AI system works
> at your job in each supported market.

## Public Goods Strategy

Public goods create trust, recruit collaborators, support the research
community, and make the lab's intellectual territory visible.

Public goods should be released when:

- they do not expose private customer data;
- they do not create avoidable operational harm;
- methodology is clear enough to prevent misuse;
- limitations are explicit;
- they improve the field's ability to reason about native adoption.

## Non-Goals

bomgoodbueno should not initially:

- build a hotel platform;
- build a generic AI chatbot product;
- build a generic translation QA platform;
- sell broad AI consulting detached from native adoption;
- claim model superiority without evidence;
- publish league tables without methodological confidence;
- chase too many languages before the first evaluation loop is reliable;
- overbuild SaaS before repeat Applied Lab workflows are known.

## Product Dependency Model

Product work depends on research work in this sequence:

```text
Mission and organization
  -> Native Adoption Framework
  -> Evaluation Framework
  -> Case/Task Decomposition
  -> AILITW Protocol
  -> AILITW-001
  -> internal research console
  -> Applied Lab operating system
  -> repeatable assurance product
```

The product must preserve:

- claim provenance;
- evidence class;
- case semantics;
- evaluator identity and role;
- traceability from finding to run;
- distinction between observation and inference;
- distinction between translated, parallel, native, and market-specific cases.

## Strategic Hypotheses

### H1: Native adoption is distinct from localization

If true, organizations need methods beyond translation QA to evaluate deployed
AI systems.

### H2: Support claims overstate parity

If true, systems that advertise a language will still show measurable gaps in
task decomposition, tool use, policy adherence, or experiential nativeness.

### H3: Case/task decomposition is a leverage point

If true, decomposition quality will predict operational success better than
surface fluency alone.

### H4: Public-system observation can identify useful gaps responsibly

If true, AILITW can generate public value while preserving ethical boundaries.

### H5: Applied assurance can fund research

If true, companies will pay for independent cross-language assurance tied to
their actual workflows.

## Unresolved Strategic Questions

- Which first market or sector provides the best applied wedge after AILITW-001?
- How public should named-system results be during the earliest studies?
- Should bomgoodbueno develop a Native Adoption Index in v0.2, or wait until
  several studies establish metric stability?
- What level of human review is necessary before commercial certification?
- Which claims should remain qualitative until the evidence system matures?
