# Organization

bomgoodbueno is an R&D lab for the native adoption of agentic systems.

The lab studies how general-purpose digital intelligence becomes genuinely
useful inside local languages, cultures, workflows, institutions, and markets.
It does not treat localization as a cosmetic layer placed on top of a globally
designed system. It treats native adoption as an operational, scientific, and
product problem.

## Core Components

### bomgoodbueno

bomgoodbueno is the institution. It owns the research philosophy, governance,
methodology, publication standards, product infrastructure, applied services,
and public-good commitments.

bomgoodbueno should be known for a precise intellectual territory:

> Native adoption of agentic systems.

### Research

Research is the scientific engine of the lab. It develops frameworks, protocols,
benchmarks, evaluation methods, and public findings. It is responsible for
separating observation from inference, claims from evidence, and hypotheses from
validated conclusions.

Research owns:

- native adoption theory;
- AILITW protocols;
- evaluation frameworks;
- case/task decomposition methods;
- language and workflow parity methods;
- evidence classes;
- publication standards.

### AILITW

AILITW means AI Living in the Wild. It is bomgoodbueno's field observatory.

AILITW observes and evaluates deployed AI systems as they exist in public,
customer-accessible, or otherwise permissioned real-world contexts. Its purpose
is to understand what agentic systems actually do when exposed to real language,
markets, claims, workflows, policies, and users.

AILITW should become the flagship research program bomgoodbueno is known for.
It is not the company. It is a program housed inside the company.

### Applied Lab

Applied Lab converts research into practical work. It helps organizations answer
questions such as:

- Does our AI system preserve capability across languages?
- Is our agent ready for a specific European market?
- Which failures are language failures, workflow failures, policy failures, or
  tool-use failures?
- Did a new release regress a supported market?
- Are vendor claims about language support equivalent to observed capability?

Applied Lab is not a generic consulting arm. It applies bomgoodbueno's research
methods to specific systems, organizations, and release decisions.

### Public Goods

Public Goods is the lab's commitment to making some knowledge, methods, and
tools reusable beyond paid work.

Public goods may include:

- public benchmark reports;
- methodology notes;
- failure taxonomies;
- anonymized or synthetic datasets;
- evaluation rubrics;
- open-source tools;
- responsible public-system testing guidance.

Public goods must not expose private customer data, violate platform terms, or
publish sensitive findings irresponsibly.

## Operating Loops

### Discovery Loop

```text
Find systems
  -> qualify public surfaces
  -> capture product and vendor claims
  -> map languages and markets
  -> build candidate cohorts
  -> register provenance
```

The Discovery Loop feeds AILITW and Applied Lab.

### Research Loop

```text
Hypothesis
  -> protocol
  -> case/task design
  -> observation
  -> evaluation
  -> failure classification
  -> finding
  -> publication
```

The Research Loop produces evidence and public credibility.

### Applied Loop

```text
Customer problem
  -> Native Adoption Analysis
  -> CompanyBench
  -> experiment
  -> remediation
  -> release assurance
  -> repeat monitoring
```

The Applied Loop creates practical value and future revenue.

### Engineering Loop

```text
Research requirement
  -> product specification
  -> implementation packet
  -> failing test
  -> implementation
  -> verification
  -> evidence
  -> documentation update
```

The Engineering Loop turns research requirements into infrastructure without
letting product convenience override scientific discipline.

## Language Network

The founding language network is deliberately lean.

The founder can initially operate across English, Spanish, and Portuguese as
used in Portugal. External certified or native reviewers may be hired case by
case through language-learning services, professional portals, translator
networks, or independent experts when a study requires additional review,
adjudication, or credibility.

The network is a shared research capability, not a department.

Roles may include:

- founder-reviewer;
- native reviewer;
- certified reviewer;
- domain reviewer;
- adjudicator;
- methodology reviewer.

Human evaluation governance is defined in
[HUMAN_EVALUATION.md](../01-research/HUMAN_EVALUATION.md).

## Boundaries

bomgoodbueno does not begin as:

- a hotel platform;
- a generic chatbot builder;
- a translation agency;
- a generic localization QA vendor;
- a model vendor;
- a synthetic benchmark factory detached from real workflows;
- a public shaming operation.

bomgoodbueno may later build software products, but product development must
serve the research and applied assurance model.

## Research-to-Product Dependency

The product roadmap depends on the research canon.

```text
Native Adoption Framework
  -> Evaluation Framework
  -> Case/Task Decomposition
  -> AILITW Protocol
  -> first studies
  -> internal research console
  -> Applied Lab workflows
  -> repeatable assurance product
```

Software should first make the lab faster, more reliable, more auditable, and
more able to produce evidence. Commercial SaaS should emerge from repeated
Applied Lab needs, not from premature platform assumptions.
