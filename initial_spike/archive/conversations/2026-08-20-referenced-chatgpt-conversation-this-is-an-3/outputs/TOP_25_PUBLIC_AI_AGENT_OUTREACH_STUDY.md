## Purpose

This study validates who and where to contact for the first 25 public AI-agent targets from the growth-led research handoff strategy.

It is designed to answer:

- Who should receive the free `Native Adoption Snapshot`?
- Which public channel should we use first?
- Which targets are likely to convert into a paid paired engagement?
- Which targets are better used as benchmark anchors rather than sales prospects?
- What should we pitch to each target?

## Key Finding

The best targets for **testing** are not always the best targets for **selling**.

General AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Meta AI, Perplexity, Mistral, Qwen, Kimi, Duck.ai, Brave Leo, and NotebookLM are useful public baselines. They are valuable for benchmark credibility, but harder to convert directly because they are large platforms with formal sales/support channels and limited incentive to engage with a cold snapshot.

The best targets for **outreach conversion** are support-agent, website-agent, and agent-builder companies:

- CoSupport
- Tidio
- Dante AI
- AnyDialog
- Helia
- Chatbase
- Botpress
- Voiceflow
- Replit
- Vercel v0
- StackBlitz/Bolt
- Lovable
- HeyGen
- ElevenLabs

These companies have direct product, sales, partner, support, or community channels, and the value proposition maps to their business: "Your product helps customers build or deploy agents. We can show where multilingual/native adoption succeeds or breaks, and give you reusable evidence for product, trust, and growth."

## Outreach Viability Scale

- `A`: strong outreach fit. Clear contact path, strong product fit, likely buyer pain.
- `B`: good fit. Contact path exists, but pitch needs sharper routing or may need warm intro.
- `C`: useful benchmark target, weak direct outreach path or low probability of response.
- `D`: keep as public benchmark only unless a warm path appears.

## Primary Outreach Motion

Use three motions:

### Motion 1: Free Snapshot To Product/Growth

Best for agent builders and CX-agent startups.

Offer:

> We ran a lightweight native-adoption snapshot on your public agent experience. Here is the packet. If useful, we can run the same method on priority customer journeys, languages, and workflows.

### Motion 2: Partner/Community Angle

Best for developer platforms and open ecosystems.

Offer:

> We are building a native-adoption benchmark and free handoff packet for multilingual AI agents. Your ecosystem is a strong fit. We would like to include a respectful snapshot and share findings privately first.

### Motion 3: Benchmark Baseline Courtesy Note

Best for large AI labs and general assistants.

Offer:

> We are using your public assistant as a baseline in a multilingual/native-adoption benchmark. We will share a scoped, non-public snapshot first, with representation limits and no broad claims.

## Recommended Message Variants

### Support/CX Agent Builder

Subject:

`Free multilingual native-adoption snapshot for {{Product}}`

Message:

```text
Hi {{Name/Team}},

We are building a research suite for native adoption of AI agents: whether multilingual users can actually understand, trust, and complete real workflows, not just whether the output is fluent.

{{Product}} is a strong fit because it helps teams deploy customer-facing AI agents. We ran/would like to run a lightweight public snapshot on one safe support journey and share the packet with you for free.

The packet includes observed failure patterns, trust/escalation notes, representation limits, and recommended next tests. No gotcha post, no broad claims, and no private data.

If useful, we can then discuss a paired sprint on your priority journeys/languages.

Would it be okay to send the snapshot to the right product or partnerships person?
```

### Developer-Agent Platform

Subject:

`Native-adoption snapshot for multilingual app-building workflows`

Message:

```text
Hi {{Team}},

We are researching how AI app-building agents perform when users describe product requirements in languages other than English, while the generated app still contains English code, UI copy, locale formats, and support flows.

{{Product}} is a natural benchmark target. We can share a free handoff packet covering one public workflow, including where multilingual product specs transfer cleanly and where user intent, UI copy, accessibility, or locale assumptions break.

This is meant as useful product feedback, not a public teardown. If the signal is useful, the paid next step is a paired sprint across your priority user journeys.
```

### Benchmark Anchor / Large Platform

Subject:

`Courtesy note: scoped multilingual native-adoption benchmark`

Message:

```text
Hi {{Team}},

We are building a scoped benchmark for native adoption of public AI assistants: whether multilingual users can complete realistic journeys with appropriate comprehension, trust calibration, locale handling, and escalation.

We plan to include {{Product}} as a public baseline. Before publishing or sharing anything external, we would like to send a narrow private snapshot with methodology, limitations, and any concerns.

This is not a certification, not a broad product claim, and not a hostile teardown.
```

## Recommended First 2-Week Outreach Study

### Day 1-2: Prepare Assets

Create:

- one sample `Native Adoption Snapshot`
- one landing page or PDF explaining the research
- outreach tracker
- three message variants
- ethical public-testing statement

### Day 3-5: Run 5 Public Snapshots

Targets:

- CoSupport
- Tidio
- Dante AI
- AnyDialog
- Chatbase

### Day 6: Send First Batch

Send:

- product/growth route
- sales/demo route if no product route
- partner/community route where available

### Day 7-10: Follow Up

Follow-up framing:

- short
- useful
- no pressure
- ask for routing to the correct owner

### Day 11-14: Evaluate

Pass criteria:

- at least 2 replies
- at least 1 meeting
- at least 1 request to send the packet or run deeper test

Strong signal:

- one target asks for private workflow testing or more languages.

Weak signal:

- replies say "interesting" but no one owns the problem.

Pivot signal:

- all replies interpret this as generic localization QA.

## Decision Recommendation

Proceed with a two-week outreach study before building more software.

Approve:

- 25-target outreach tracker
- 5 initial free snapshots
- product/growth-first outreach
- partner/community routes where sales is too formal

Do not approve yet:

- mass outbound to all 25
- public teardown posts
- automated high-volume testing
- claims that any target "fails" native adoption

The first validated milestone is not revenue. It is:

```text
5 packets created
5 packets sent
2 replies
1 meeting
1 concrete request for deeper testing
```
