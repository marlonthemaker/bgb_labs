## Purpose

This document reframes the conservative pre-build plan into an aggressive startup growth motion while preserving research integrity, trust, and product discipline.

## Core Strategy

Use upfront research as the growth engine.

The motion is:

```text
public/low-friction AI-in-the-wild research
  -> free high-value handoff packet
  -> buyer recognizes specific adoption gaps
  -> paid paired engagement
  -> repeatable treatment and benchmark assets
  -> productized workflow
```

The customer-facing posture:

> Nice to meet you. We studied how your AI agent experience works for multilingual users. Here is a useful, evidence-backed handoff packet for free. You only pay if you want help interpreting, extending, fixing, retesting, or operationalizing it.

## Why This Can Work

This is a strong startup wedge because it creates value before asking for trust.

It works if the free packet is:

- specific enough to feel real
- rigorous enough to be credible
- limited enough to avoid unpaid consulting
- actionable enough to create urgency
- scoped enough to avoid legal, privacy, or reputational blowback

The free product is not the full assessment. The free product is the proof of insight.

## Free Handoff Packet

### Packet Name

`Native Adoption Snapshot`

### Contents

- product or agent surface reviewed
- language/locale/community context tested
- journey tested
- observed adoption failures
- failure taxonomy mapping
- screenshots or public artifacts where allowed
- risk and trust notes
- representation limits
- likely business impact
- recommended next tests
- recommended treatment directions
- clear "what we did not test" section

### What It Should Not Include For Free

- full benchmark suite
- private evaluator operations
- raw sensitive evidence
- custom treatment implementation
- extensive retesting
- deep competitive analysis
- launch readiness certification
- customer-specific legal or compliance claim
- unlimited meetings

## Paid Conversion Offer

### Offer Name

`Paired Native Adoption Sprint`

### Paid Scope

- 2 to 4 week engagement
- one to three critical journeys
- three to five language/locale/community contexts
- baseline runs
- evaluator packet
- failure map
- treatment backlog
- one treatment prototype or implementation spec
- retest
- executive decision memo
- technical artifact handoff

### Conversion Trigger

Convert when the customer says one of:

- "Can you test our actual workflow?"
- "Can you tell us how we compare?"
- "Can you help fix this?"
- "Can we show this to product/support leadership?"
- "Can this become a readiness gate?"
- "Can you run this across more languages?"

## Growth Loops

### Loop 1: Public Teardown Loop

1. Select public AI agent/product experience.
2. Run a lightweight native adoption snapshot.
3. Publish a respectful anonymized or public-facing mini-analysis.
4. Offer the company a private handoff packet.
5. Convert interested teams into paired sprint.

Best for:

- SaaS products
- support bots
- travel agents
- productivity agents
- public AI assistants

Risk:

- can feel adversarial if framed as "gotcha."

Mitigation:

- frame as free research and improvement opportunity.

### Loop 2: Benchmark Invitation Loop

1. Announce a focused benchmark theme.
2. Invite companies to be included.
3. Give each participant a free private snapshot.
4. Publish aggregate learnings without exposing sensitive details.
5. Sell deeper assessment, treatment, and retesting.

Best for:

- trust-building
- partner ecosystem
- thought leadership

Risk:

- too slow if overdesigned.

Mitigation:

- start with one journey and one domain.

### Loop 3: Partner Channel Loop

1. Give LSPs, localization teams, CX consultancies, and AI governance advisors a free framework.
2. Let them identify accounts with multilingual AI friction.
3. Offer co-branded snapshots.
4. Convert into paid paired sprints.

Best for:

- distribution
- credibility
- access to buyers

Risk:

- partners may want white-label control too early.

Mitigation:

- keep first partner offers lightweight and evidence-standardized.

### Loop 4: Free Tool Loop

1. Release a lightweight checklist or CLI validator.
2. Let teams self-assess journeys, evidence fields, and representation statements.
3. Offer expert review of their packet.
4. Convert to paid sprint.

Best for:

- product-led credibility
- developer/eval engineer trust

Risk:

- building tools before buyer pull.

Mitigation:

- start with templates before CLI.

## Ideal First Wedge

Start with customer support or CX agents.

Why:

- clear business metrics
- multilingual support pain is obvious
- AI deployment budgets exist
- public support experiences can sometimes be inspected
- failures are easy to explain to executives

Initial benchmark theme:

> Can AI support agents resolve common account, billing, or setup issues natively across languages without confusing trust, consent, escalation, or local context?

## First 90 Days

### Days 1-15: Build The Free Packet

Create:

- `Native Adoption Snapshot` template
- journey intake template
- screenshot/evidence policy
- representation statement template
- 1-page offer for paid paired sprint

Target:

- one journey
- one domain
- three languages or locales
- public or low-risk surfaces only

### Days 16-30: Run 5 Snapshots

Run lightweight snapshots on:

- 3 public AI support or onboarding experiences
- 2 friendly/design-partner candidates

Goal:

- prove the packet creates "oh wow, this is useful" reaction.

Metric:

- 3 of 5 recipients agree to a follow-up conversation.

### Days 31-60: Convert First Paid Or Deep Partner Sprint

Do:

- 20 targeted outreach messages
- 10 discovery calls
- 3 handoff packet meetings
- 1 paid or formal design-partner sprint

Metric:

- at least one customer gives access to real workflow artifacts.

### Days 61-90: Package Repeatability

Create:

- v1 failure pattern library
- v1 paid sprint statement of work
- v1 artifact checklist
- v1 customer integration checklist
- v1 anonymized case memo

Metric:

- one repeatable packet format
- one repeatable offer
- one validated buyer segment

## Outreach Positioning

### Short Version

We run native adoption snapshots for AI agents: not translation QA, but whether multilingual users can actually understand, trust, and complete real agentic workflows. We prepared a free handoff packet for your team. If useful, we can help run a deeper paired sprint and treatment retest.

### More Direct Version

Your AI experience may be fluent in other languages but still fail at consent, escalation, local context, or tool handoff. We run lightweight native adoption checks and give teams a free evidence-backed packet. You only pay if you want help expanding, fixing, or operationalizing it.

### Partner Version

We help localization, CX, and AI trust teams move beyond translation quality into native adoption readiness for AI agents. We can provide a free snapshot for one workflow, then co-develop a paid improvement sprint if the signal is strong.

## Sellability Logic

The free packet sells because it reduces buyer uncertainty.

It answers:

- Is this problem real in our product?
- Is this team credible?
- What would we do next?
- How could this affect launch, support, trust, or expansion?
- Is the paid engagement likely to produce useful decisions?

The paid sprint sells because the free packet deliberately stops before:

- complete coverage
- implementation
- retesting
- internal stakeholder alignment
- benchmark comparison
- readiness decision

### Recurring

`Native Adoption Readiness Program`

## Guardrails

Do not:

- publicly shame companies
- claim broad language failure from one snapshot
- handle private user data without agreement
- imply certification
- give away full treatment implementation for free
- let the free work become bespoke consulting

Do:

- be generous with insight
- be narrow with claims
- show evidence
- state representation limits
- make the next paid step obvious
- track conversion and repeatability

## Success Metrics

### Growth Metrics

- snapshots created per month
- handoff packets sent
- handoff calls booked
- conversion to paid sprint
- design partners recruited
- referral rate

### Value Metrics

- failures discovered that customer did not know
- customer decisions influenced
- treatment backlog items accepted
- retests completed
- support/product/trust metric tied to finding

### Product Metrics

- time to produce snapshot
- repeatable fields across packets
- percent of packet generated from schemas
- number of reusable failure patterns
- number of reusable treatment patterns

## Strategic Recommendation

The conservative plan and aggressive growth plan are compatible.

Use the conservative plan as the quality system.

Use the free handoff packet as the growth engine.

Do not wait for the full platform. Build just enough artifact infrastructure to produce credible snapshots quickly, then let customer pull decide which integration surfaces deserve engineering investment.
