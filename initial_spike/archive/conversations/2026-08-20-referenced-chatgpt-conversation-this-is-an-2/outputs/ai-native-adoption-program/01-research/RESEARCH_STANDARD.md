# Research Standard

ID: `DOC-RES-0001`
Version: v0.1

## Purpose

This standard defines how work becomes credible inside the program.

## Required Elements

Each research item must include:

- a stable ID
- track ownership
- question or hypothesis
- scope and exclusions
- method
- evidence sources
- evaluator assumptions
- risk and limitation notes
- decision impact

## Evidence Grades

- Grade A: replicated across track boundaries or independent datasets.
- Grade B: reproduced within one track under controlled conditions.
- Grade C: plausible single-run evidence with clear limitations.
- Grade D: exploratory observation only.

## Claim Format

Use this pattern:

`FIND-<TRACK>-####`: Under `<conditions>`, `<system/population>` showed `<effect>`, supported by `<evidence IDs>`, with `<limitations>`.

## Minimum Review

Any public or roadmap-shaping claim needs:

- at least one reviewer outside the originating track
- direct links to evidence
- a stated confidence level
- a decision recommendation or explicit note that no decision is warranted
