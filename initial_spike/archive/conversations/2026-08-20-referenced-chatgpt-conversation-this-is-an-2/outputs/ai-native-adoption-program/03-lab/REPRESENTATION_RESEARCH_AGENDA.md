ID: `DOC-LAB-0001`

## Purpose

Lab investigates mechanisms that may explain multilingual behavior observed in Wild and affected by Sandbox treatments.

## Initial Workstreams

- Cross-language intent consistency for agent tool calls.
- Representation stability for uncertainty and safety concepts.
- Prompt-language versus task-language mismatch.
- Locale-sensitive entity grounding.
- Error repair behavior after multilingual misunderstanding.

## Inputs From Wild

Lab should prioritize failures with high severity, high frequency, or unclear cause. Each Lab question should link to `FAIL-*`, `OBS-*`, or `EVT-*` records.

## Outputs To Sandbox

Lab should produce candidate mechanisms and treatment recommendations, such as routing policies, clarification prompts, uncertainty templates, or locale-aware retrieval checks.
