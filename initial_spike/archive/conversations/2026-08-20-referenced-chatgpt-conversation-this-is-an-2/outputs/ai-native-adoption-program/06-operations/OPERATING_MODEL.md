# Operating Model

ID: `DOC-OPS-0001`
Version: v0.2

## Cadence

- Weekly research review: evidence, findings, and blocked experiments.
- Weekly build review: runner, registries, Sandbox prototypes, platform issues.
- Biweekly decision review: scope, language coverage, risk, and roadmap changes.
- Monthly synthesis: update thesis, roadmap, and public-facing narrative.

## Roles

- Program lead: owns thesis, prioritization, and cross-track synthesis.
- Wild lead: owns journeys, field observations, benchmark candidates.
- Lab lead: owns diagnostic research and mechanism hypotheses.
- Sandbox lead: owns interventions, treatments, and adaptation prototypes.
- Platform lead: owns evidence substrate, runner, schemas, and automation.
- Evaluation lead: owns rubrics, evaluator training, and review quality.
- Trust lead: owns evidence integrity, evaluator reliability, privacy, safety, and claim readiness review.
- Product lead: owns capability packaging, offer design, readiness gates, and value-generation evidence.
- Locale/community review lead: owns language, locale, accessibility, and representation-quality review for selected communities.

## Workflow

Use `PRODUCT_DELIVERY_LIFECYCLE.md` as the canonical lifecycle.

Summary:

1. Frame.
2. Register.
3. Baseline.
4. Diagnose.
5. Treat.
6. Retest.
7. Decide.
8. Package.

## Governance

Roadmap-shaping decisions must be logged in `DECISION_LOG.md`. Major unproven premises must be logged in `ASSUMPTION_REGISTER.md`.

Document approvals, editability rules, review cadence, and archival behavior are governed by `DOCUMENT_LIFECYCLE.md`.

## Management Review

Before a benchmark release, product pilot, or external claim, review:

- current findings and evidence grade
- active risks and mitigations
- evaluator reliability notes
- representation limits
- accessibility considerations
- unsupported claims
- readiness gate status
- corrective actions from prior reviews

Review outputs should include:

- decision ID
- approved scope
- blocked claims or artifacts
- required mitigations
- owner and due date

## Document Governance

Before adding or editing a document, identify its lifecycle class:

- constitutional
- standard or method
- architecture or design
- living operating record
- study definition
- study output or evidence record
- human-authored report
- generated view or derived artifact

Use amendments or superseding documents for locked, released, append-only, or archived artifacts.
