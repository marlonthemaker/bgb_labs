## Purpose

This domain owns trust, safety, validity, governance, and evidence integrity across research and product work.

## Scope

- evidence quality controls
- evaluator reliability
- privacy and consent requirements
- benchmark validity
- cultural harm and misuse review
- auditability standards for internal and customer-facing claims

## Boundary

This domain defines what makes evidence and claims trustworthy. It does not own the research questions, benchmark implementation, or product offer.
