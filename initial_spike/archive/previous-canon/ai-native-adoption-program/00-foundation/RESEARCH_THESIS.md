## Thesis

Native adoption is the ability of a user to use an agentic system in their own language and context with comparable discoverability, comprehension, task success, trust calibration, and agency to a default-language user.

The program studies native adoption as a system property. It may depend on model representations, prompts, agent orchestration, tools, memory, retrieval, product copy, cultural assumptions, and the user's expectations.

## Inverse Problem

Wild observes failures after deployment. Lab asks what underlying behavior may explain them. Sandbox asks whether a practical external treatment can reduce them without internal access.

This creates an inverse research loop:

- observation: a user fails or drops off
- diagnosis: identify likely failure mechanism
- intervention: apply a black-box adaptation
- validation: retest adoption under matched conditions
