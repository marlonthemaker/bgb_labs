# Program Charter

ID: `DOC-FOUND-0001`
Version: v0.1

## Mission

Build a research and product system that measures, explains, and improves native adoption of multilingual agentic AI systems.

## Core Question

Can agentic systems work natively for multilingual users, not merely produce translated text?

## Program Hypotheses

The canonical hypothesis definitions live in `01-research/HYPOTHESIS_REGISTER.md`.

Initial thesis IDs:

- `HYP-ALL-0001`
- `HYP-ALL-0002`
- `HYP-SBOX-0001`

## Tracks

- Wild: observe adoption in realistic contexts.
- Lab: diagnose causes and mechanisms.
- Sandbox: test external interventions and treatments.

## Non-Goals

- Ranking every model in every language.
- Reducing multilingual quality to translation quality.
- Claiming causality from Wild observations alone.
- Treating cultural fit as a cosmetic localization problem.

## Success Definition

The program succeeds when it can turn a multilingual user failure into a documented diagnosis, a tested intervention, and a retested adoption improvement.
