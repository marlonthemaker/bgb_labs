# Risk Register

ID: `DOC-OPS-0003`
Version: v0.2

## Active Risks

`RISK-OPS-0001`: Over-broad scope.

- Impact: slow progress and unclear findings.
- Mitigation: start with one closed loop before expanding.

`RISK-OPS-0002`: Confusing translation quality with native adoption.

- Impact: wrong metrics and misleading conclusions.
- Mitigation: include journey, trust, comprehension, and continuation measures.

`RISK-OPS-0003`: Human evaluator inconsistency.

- Impact: noisy or biased findings.
- Mitigation: define rubrics, preserve disagreement, calibrate reviewers.

`RISK-OPS-0004`: Black-box treatments create hidden regressions.

- Impact: improved language feel but worse accuracy, safety, or agency.
- Mitigation: require baseline comparison and regression checks.

`RISK-OPS-0005`: Sensitive multilingual user data.

- Impact: privacy and trust harm.
- Mitigation: classify evidence sensitivity and define retention policy before collection.

`RISK-OPS-0006`: Provider-specific results overgeneralized.

- Impact: findings do not transfer.
- Mitigation: state system scope and retest across systems before broad claims.

`RISK-OPS-0007`: Convenience samples mistaken for community representation.

- Impact: product claims or findings may misrepresent real language communities.
- Mitigation: require representation statements, evaluator fit notes, and explicit non-representation limits.

`RISK-OPS-0008`: Accessibility gaps hidden by fluent language output.

- Impact: users with disabilities, low literacy, device constraints, or low bandwidth may still fail the journey.
- Mitigation: evaluate accessibility context where relevant and include WCAG-aligned checks for product surfaces.

`RISK-OPS-0009`: Product value overclaimed from research prototypes.

- Impact: misleading commercialization and loss of trust.
- Mitigation: require readiness gates, value claim levels, and trust review before customer-facing claims.

`RISK-OPS-0010`: Governance records become performative.

- Impact: the program appears rigorous while decisions are still driven by anecdotes or urgency.
- Mitigation: require decision IDs, linked evidence, reversal conditions, and corrective actions for major reviews.

`RISK-OPS-0011`: Locale data errors conflated with cultural judgment.

- Impact: fixes may target the wrong layer and create worse cultural or usability failures.
- Mitigation: separate locale data errors, cultural fit errors, product localization errors, and model behavior errors.

`RISK-OPS-0012`: Affected communities treated only as market segments.

- Impact: extractive research, reputational harm, and weaker product legitimacy.
- Mitigation: add community review for high-impact or underrepresented contexts and preserve representation limits in claims.
